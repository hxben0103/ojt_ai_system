[Topic: Absence Policy]
1. Excused Absences: An absence is only considered excused if it is due to sickness (supported by a valid medical certificate) or force majeure (calamities/natural disasters).
2. Notification: In case of sickness or unforeseen incidents, the student must inform their immediate supervisor at the host training establishment, the OJT Supervisor/Instructor, and the Team Leader at least one hour before their official start time.
3. Documentation: A medical certificate or relevant documentation must be submitted upon return.
4. Demerits: Unexcused absences reflect poorly on the final evaluation and may incur demerits in the OJT grading system.
