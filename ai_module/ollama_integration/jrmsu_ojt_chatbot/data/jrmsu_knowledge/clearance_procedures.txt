[Topic: Clearance and Completion Procedures]
1. Narrative Report: Upon completion of the required OJT hours, students must submit a comprehensive Narrative Report documenting their daily activities, learnings, and reflections.
2. Daily Time Record (DTR): All DTRs must be signed by the host company supervisor and submitted for verification of hours completed.
3. Evaluation Forms: Both the Host Training Establishment (HTE) supervisor and the OJT coordinator must complete an evaluation of the student's performance.
4. Final Clearance: Final OJT clearance is granted only after the submission of the Narrative Report, signed DTR, and completed evaluation forms. Students should ensure all deficiencies are cleared before the end of the semester.
