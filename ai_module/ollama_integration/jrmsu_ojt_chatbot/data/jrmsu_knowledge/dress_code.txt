[Topic: Dress Code and Grooming]
1. Proper Grooming: OJT students must observe proper grooming at all times during their practicum. This includes neat hair, clean nails, and overall professional presentation.
2. Attire: Unless a specific uniform is provided by the host training establishment, students should wear appropriate professional or business attire according to industry standards.
3. University Identity: Students should always carry their university ID and, if required, wear the specified OJT uniform or school uniform on designated days.
4. Professionalism: Attire should be modest and reflect the professional standards of Jose Rizal Memorial State University.
