[Topic: Late Attendance Policy]
1. Tardiness and Log-in/out: OJT trainees are required to log in at least 15 minutes before their official start time and log out 15 minutes after their official end time at the industry or school site.
2. Consequences of Tardiness: If a student accumulates excessive minutes of tardiness or multiple instances of being late, they may be required to perform an eight-hour make-up duty.
3. Habitual Tardiness: Habitual tardiness is subject to demerits and may result in a formal meeting with the OJT coordinator.
