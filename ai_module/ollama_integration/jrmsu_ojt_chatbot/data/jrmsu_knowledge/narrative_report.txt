The narrative report consists of three chapters. Chapter one includes the host training establishment profile such as its overview, brief history, logo and meaning, core values, philosophy, vision, mission, goals, and organizational chart. Chapter two contains training activities which include narrative experiences, weekly progress reports, Daily Time Record, documentation with photos and captions, evaluation sheet, and certificate of completion. Chapter three presents results and findings such as accomplishments, competencies gained, outputs, and impact on the student’s career. Appendices include registrar documents, certificates, medical documents, parent consent forms, letters of acceptance, application letters, resumes, and the Memorandum of Agreement.
