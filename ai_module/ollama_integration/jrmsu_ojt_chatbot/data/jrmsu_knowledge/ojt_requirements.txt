To participate in the On-the-Job Training (OJT) program at JRMSU, students must be enrolled under the College of Computing Studies programs and satisfy the required OJT hours. The Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (BSCS) requires a minimum of 200 hours of OJT in an IT-related organization, while the Bachelor of Science in Information Systems (BSIS) requires a minimum of 480 hours of OJT in an IT-related organization.
Students must identify a suitable Host Training Establishment (HTE) that can provide IT-related work experience and coordinate with the OJT Coordinator for approval. A Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) between JRMSU and the host establishment is required and must be approved by the University President. Multiple notarized copies of the MOA are usually prepared.
Before deployment, students must secure and submit several requirements, which commonly include a Parent’s Waiver and Consent Form, a Recommendation Letter for OJT deployment issued by the CCS Practicum Coordinator and noted by the Associate Dean, a Letter of Acceptance from the host establishment, a Medical Certificate and required laboratory results, a Student Copy from the Registrar, and the student’s Resume and Application Letter. During the OJT, students must maintain a Daily Time Record (DTR), participate in orientation and training, perform assigned tasks, and later submit a Narrative Report and other required documents for evaluation and completion of the program.
