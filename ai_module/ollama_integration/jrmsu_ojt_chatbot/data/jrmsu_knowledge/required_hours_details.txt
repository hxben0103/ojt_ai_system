[Topic: Required Hours Details]
1. Total Hours: The required number of OJT hours varies by program. Most programs require between 200 to 400 hours of hands-on training. 
2. Program Specifics: For example, Bachelor of Science in Accounting Technology requires a minimum of 320 hours. Other programs may range from 240 to 600 hours depending on the curriculum year.
3. Daily Limits: Students are generally expected to follow regular industry hours but should not exceed 10 hours of training in a single day unless specifically permitted by the coordinator and supervisor.
4. Overtime: Excessive overtime should be avoided to prevent fatigue, though some departments allow make-up hours through overtime with prior approval.
